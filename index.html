Info Apparel Resume

I design and build for humans, whether they’re wearing my work or tapping through it.

6+ years as an apparel designer taught me to own a product end to end, from first sketch to factory floor, with deep empathy for bodies, behaviours, and needs. Now I bring that same ownership to digital products: I research, design and build, taking ideas all the way to a live website.

I bridge physical and digital, bringing a tactile understanding of form and fit into intuitive interfaces. My belief? Design should be functional, beautiful, and so considerate of its users that it just works. Making things by hand taught me that. Cutting a pattern, draping fabric or fitting a sample puts me in a kind of flow where the next step reveals itself, and I feel the same thing when I build an interface: the best screens, like the best garments, feel simple until you look closely at how they are made. This site is my proof. I designed it, wrote it and built it myself, from the first sketch to the live page you are reading now.
